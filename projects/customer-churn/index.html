Customer Churn Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis of Telco Customer Churn

An analysis of customer churn patterns in a Telco customer dataset, exploring which customer characteristics and service factors are associated with retention and churn.

01 / Overview

Project overview

This project uses a Telco customer churn dataset to explore customer behaviour and identify patterns associated with churn.

The analysis covers data inspection, data cleaning, exploratory analysis and visualisation, carried out in Python within a Jupyter Notebook.

02 / Business problem

The business question

Which customer characteristics and service factors are associated with higher customer churn?

Losing customers is costly for subscription businesses. Understanding which groups churn more often helps decide where retention efforts and further investigation should be focused.

03 / Dataset

The dataset

A Telco customer churn dataset containing customer-level service and account information, together with each customer’s churn status.

  • Churn Churn status — the target of the analysis
  • Contract Contract type
  • InternetService Internet service type
  • PaymentMethod Payment method
  • tenure Length of time as a customer (months)
  • MonthlyCharges Monthly charge amount
  • TotalCharges Total amount charged

04 / Data cleaning

Data inspection & cleaning

Before analysis, the data was inspected and the TotalCharges column was cleaned so it could be used as a numeric field.

  1. Inspected the data

    Reviewed the data shape, column names and data types.

  2. Checked data quality

    Checked for missing values and duplicate records.

  3. Cleaned TotalCharges

    Stripped whitespace, converted invalid or empty values to missing values, and converted the column to numeric using coercion.

08 / Business insights

Interpreting the patterns

What the observed patterns may indicate. These are associations found through exploratory analysis and suggest areas for further investigation — they do not show that any factor causes churn.

Early tenure

Churned customers have a much lower average tenure. This may indicate that customers in their early months would benefit from stronger onboarding and engagement.

Contract length

Longer contracts are associated with lower churn in this dataset, with month-to-month customers showing the highest churn rate.

Fiber optic customers

Higher churn is observed among fiber optic customers. This suggests an area for further investigation, such as service quality or pricing.

Electronic check payments

Customers paying by electronic check show higher churn than those using automated methods such as bank transfer or credit card.

Monthly charges

Higher monthly charges are associated with churn in the analysis. This may indicate price sensitivity, which would need further analysis to confirm.

09 / Recommendations

Recommendations

Suggested next steps documented in the project, based on the observed patterns.

  1. Focus retention efforts on early-stage customers.
  2. Promote longer-term contract options.
  3. Investigate the fiber optic customer segment.
  4. Encourage migration from electronic check towards automated payment methods.
  5. Investigate service quality or pricing concerns in higher-churn segments.

12 / Conclusion

Conclusion

The analysis identified several customer segments and account characteristics associated with different churn rates, including tenure, contract type, internet service, payment method and monthly charges.

As an exploratory analysis, these findings describe patterns rather than causes. They provide a starting point for further investigation and for more detailed retention analysis.
